# 📋 Project Overview — Blindspot AI

> **Hackathon:** CoNSTruct 2.0 — Replit × Newton School of Technology
> **Duration:** 1 Month
> **GitHub:** [Antrikshcodes](https://github.com/Antrikshcodes)

---

## Vision

Blindspot AI leverages artificial intelligence to **reveal blind spots in decision-making** — the hidden biases, overlooked data points, and cognitive gaps that lead to suboptimal outcomes. By surfacing what you don't see, Blindspot AI empowers users to make more informed, balanced, and effective decisions.

---

## Goals & Objectives

1. **Identify Decision Blind Spots** — Build an AI-powered engine that analyzes decisions and highlights overlooked factors, biases, and risks.
2. **Provide Actionable Insights** — Go beyond detection to offer concrete suggestions for improving decision quality.
3. **Intuitive User Experience** — Create a clean, accessible interface that makes complex AI analysis easy to understand.
4. **Real-Time Analysis** — Deliver fast, responsive feedback so users can iterate on their decisions quickly.
5. **Demonstrate Technical Excellence** — Showcase a well-architected, production-quality application within the hackathon timeframe.
